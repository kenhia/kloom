On 20 July 1969 the Apollo 11 lunar module _Eagle_ landed in the Sea of
Tranquility, and Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin became the first people to
walk on another world while Michael Collins orbited overhead.

The mission rested on centuries of mathematics, printing and instrument
making — a civilisation's accumulated record, turned outward.
