When I get my yearly flu shot, I text Kathy. I send the text to her not in her
capacity as a nurse (retired) but in her capacity as my Mom.

During her service with the Navy, she pushed the boundaries of what a Navy
nurse could do, and I'm sure her example opened doors for the nurses who came
after her. Our relationship was built mostly after she retired (my fault, not
hers), and my soul is better for it.

She is a master gardener who takes time to snap photos of interesting fungi to
send me; I have a black thumb but reciprocate with photos of dandelions and
other wildflowers. She got me hooked on following the UConn women's basketball
team, and for me March Madness has zero to do with the men's tournament.

I'm proud of both of my parents: their accomplishments in service to our
country and the people they are. But Dad didn't have a say in the matter;
Kathy did, and I am grateful that she decided to be my Mom.

K.
